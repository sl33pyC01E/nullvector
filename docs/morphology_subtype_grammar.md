# Morphology subtype chassis grammar

The production neural bank proves strong five-family silhouette separation but
only weak separation among its four subtypes per family. The additive
`forge.morphology_subtype_grammar` compiler turns those subtype labels into
explicit, named chassis priors before the existing renderer creates categorical
fields.

The twenty forms are:

- humanoid: strider, guardian, seer, brute;
- animalian: runner, pouncer, grazer, crawler;
- plantlike: sapling, canopy, vine, bulb;
- anomaly: monolith, rift, star, tendril;
- machine: drone, walker, siege, utility.

Each form owns a materially different body proportion, head scale, paired limb
plan, stance, auxiliary appendage reach, armor depth, weapon reach, and core
scale. Small deterministic identity jitter remains inside every form. Chassis
and primary appendages use a soft bilateral prior: perfect mirroring is not
required, but subtype identity no longer depends on a randomly broken side.

The compiler deliberately calls the existing morphology renderer and strict
specimen validator. Consequently every result retains the current 48px safety
margin, connected structural union, semantic layers, joints, sockets, training
fields, role conditioning, palette contract, and graph-driven motion inputs.
It is an additive future-corpus authority; the sealed production neural bank is
not silently rewritten.

```powershell
python -m forge.morphology_subtype_grammar build
python -m forge.morphology_subtype_grammar validate `
  outputs/morphology_subtype_grammar_v1/morphology_subtype_grammar.json
```

The immutable bank contains 160 specimens: eight identities/roles for every
one of twenty subtypes. Its gates require contract validity, unique semantics,
at least 75% leave-one-out four-way shape accuracy within every family, at
least 50% recall for every individual subtype, and a minimum family-level mean
horizontal symmetry of 0.68. The contact sheet is native nearest-neighbor pixel
art and is byte-replayed with the manifest.
