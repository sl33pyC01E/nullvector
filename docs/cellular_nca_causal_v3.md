# Long-horizon causal cellular NCA

V2 learned the direction of all four organ failures but was rejected: its
two-step curriculum underfit respiration and digestion at sixteen recurrent
steps and over-amplified neural collapse. V3 warm-starts that preserved model
and trains six recurrent updates with matched counterfactual supervision at
steps two, four, and six.

The production gate is unchanged. All four ablations must reduce their own
readout, causal error must beat the original NCA by 25%, and general physiology
must remain within the existing health, fluid, and neural tolerances.
