# Current anatomical organism VAE

V6 preserves the V5 anatomical attention weights but starts a new immutable
lineage against the current developmental corpus. The old V5 lineage remains
untouched evidence: its corpus predates the latest chassis and appendage work.

Each segment is 100 steps, writes through a unique staging directory, records
its predecessor, and binds the exact current corpus hash. No checkpoint is
promoted without a new held-out five-family motion evaluation.
